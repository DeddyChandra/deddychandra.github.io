宋大峰祖师 (1039–1127) — the Song-dynasty monk who built the bridge at Hepin (和平) across the Lian River in Chaoyang and gave out medicine, burials and famine relief to the poor. Locals raised 报德堂 in his memory: the first charity hall (善堂) in Chaoshan, and the ancestor of hundreds more across Teochew communities in Hong Kong, Thailand, Singapore and Indonesia. His birthday falls on the 29th day of the 10th lunar month.

How this works: dates are derived at page load with lunar-javascript — Chinese New Year is lunar 1/1, 大峰祖师圣诞 is lunar 10/29. Nothing is hardcoded, so occurrences that have already passed drop off the list automatically and the next ten always roll forward. Countdowns target 00:00 WIB of each date regardless of your device timezone.
