Light one up

This experience uses your webcam to track your hand and mouth. Nothing is uploaded — everything runs locally in your browser.

  1. Pinch your index finger and thumb together to hold the cigarette.
  2. Light it: make a fist with your thumb up on your other hand (a lighter) and touch the flame to the tip.
  3. Bring it to your lips and open your mouth to exhale smoke. It burns down with every puff.
  4. Throw it with a quick flick of your hand — any time — and a fresh one is dealt.

No camera? Try the mouse demo (hold mouse = pinch, hold L = lighter)
