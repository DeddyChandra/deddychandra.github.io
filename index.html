About me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Traveloka on the Financial Services & Corporate Travel teams. For 4+ years I've designed and built systems that handle real money and real travel for thousands of corporate clients — from Flight Insurance add-ons to Loan Repayment workflows to automated invoicing.

My day-to-day mixes Spring Boot, microservices, event-driven architectures, and AWS infrastructure managed via Terraform. I care a lot about system design, query performance, secure dependencies, and mentoring teammates into sharper engineers.

Before Traveloka I interned at DNArtworks shipping brand sites for Vespa and Piaggio, and spent two years assisting at Parahyangan Catholic University. I'm an ICPC Asia Regional alum (Rank 10) and a continuous AWS learner.
